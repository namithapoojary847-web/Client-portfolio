Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need lab reports before my first session?

Not required, but if you have recent blood work it helps build your plan faster. If you don't, we can start with a nutrition history and go from there.

Are sessions online or in person?

Both — most ongoing check-ins happen over video, and the first consult can be in person or online, whichever works for you.

How long until I see results?

It depends on the condition, but most patients notice early changes in energy and digestion within 2–4 weeks, with lab markers improving over 2–3 months.

Do you work with existing medical conditions?

Yes — plans are built to work alongside guidance from your physician or endocrinologist, not in place of it.
